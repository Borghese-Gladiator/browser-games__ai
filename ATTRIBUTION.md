# Attribution

## Mahjong tile artwork — `public/tiles/`

The 34 suit and honour tiles, and the tile back, come from
[FluffyStuff/riichi-mahjong-tiles](https://github.com/FluffyStuff/riichi-mahjong-tiles)
(the `Regular` variant). Those assets are released into the public domain under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

The 8 flower tiles are not part of that set, because a riichi set has none. This
repo draws them on the same set's blank tile front and adds the traditional
character: 春 夏 秋 冬 梅 蘭 菊 竹. Those files are therefore also CC0.

`bin/fetch-tiles.mjs` regenerates the whole directory.
